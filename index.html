About

Eight-plus years across film & music.

Ashell is a highly skilled audio engineer, music producer, and multi-instrumentalist. Originally from La Paz, Baja California Sur, Mexico, he moved to Denver to advance his expertise in media arts.

He's now an assistant engineer at Colorado Sound Studios and founder of Fox Audio, LLC, in Denver. At CU Denver, he co-designed, co-developed, and co-taught MUSC 4820 — Digital Music Techniques, a course on modular synthesis and sound design, and continues teaching audio production at CU Denver, Northern Virginia Community College, and Pickering Creative Artists Academy.
