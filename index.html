About

Over a decade of experience across film and music.

Ashell is an audio engineer, music producer, and multi-instrumentalist. Originally from La Paz, Baja California Sur, Mexico, he moved to Denver to advance his expertise in media arts and now runs Fox Audio, LLC independently. His work spans music production, mixing and mastering, audio post-production for film, and sound design, including work as an assistant engineer at Colorado Sound Studios.

He's worked with clients across the U.S. and internationally, including Colombia, Argentina, Mexico, and Guatemala. As an educator, he has taught audio production at CU Denver and Northern Virginia Community College.
